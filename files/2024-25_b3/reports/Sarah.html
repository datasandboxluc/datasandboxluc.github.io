Who is Ed Sheeran?

Ed Sheeran’s Mathematics Album

Plus = signifies the beginning of something new and exciting. Potential growth, creativity, and the opening of doors.

Multiply = symbolizing growth and expansion.

Divide = symbolizes the dividing of his past from his present and future as an artist. It also reflects Ed’s desire to look at life from different perspectives and the balancing act between his personal and professional life

Album analysis

Ed Sheeran is well-known for albums named after mathematical symbols. The behind stories to the album titles are that

Boxplot for Valence levels
df$album_name <- as.factor(df$album_name)
ggplot(df, aes(x = album_name, y = valence, color = album_release_year)) + 
  geom_boxplot(fill = "white") + 
  labs(x = "Album name", y = "Valence", title = "Valence Level for Each Album") + 
  theme(plot.title = element_text(hjust = 0.5))

The average valence level for each album are quite similar. Album ‘=’ has the highest valence level and album ‘-’ has the lowest valence level. These two albums are most recently released mathematical album. I wonder why his valence level had such a drastic change between 2022 and 2023!

Boxplot for Energy Level
# Load ggplot2
ggplot(df,aes(x=album_name, y=energy))+ 
    geom_boxplot(fill = "light blue")+ 
    labs(title = "Energy level for Each Album", x="Album name", y = "energy")+
    theme(plot.title = element_text(hjust = 0.5))

Ed Sheeran’s % (divide) album has the highest average of energy level. Some of the high energy songs in the % album include: Castle on the Hill and New Man.

Whereas, Ed Sheeran’s = (equal) album has the lowest average of energy level. Some of the low energy songs in the = album include: The Joker and the Queen, First Times

by the way, he sang The Joker and the Queen with Taylor Swift! check it out!

Scatter Plot on : Energy VS Valence
ggplot(df, aes(x = danceability, y = energy, color = album_name)) +
  geom_point() +
  geom_smooth(method = "lm", se = FALSE, color = "black") +  # Linear regression line
  labs(title = "Energy vs Dance-ability") +
  theme(plot.title = element_text(hjust = 0.5))

From this scatter plot and regression line, we can find a negative correlation between dance-ability and energy. In other words, Ed Sheeran’s songs with lower energy level may be suitable for dancing. This graph reminded me of his song Perfect which is a classic slow dance song!
