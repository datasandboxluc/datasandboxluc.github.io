Who are the Arctic Monkeys

Arctic Monkeys are a British rock band from Sheffield, known for their sharp lyrics, dynamic sound, and ever-evolving style. Rising to fame with Whatever People Say I Am, That’s What I’m Not in 2006, they became one of the most influential indie rock bands of the 21st century. Their music blends garage rock, post-punk, and psychedelic influences, with albums like AM bringing them global stardom. Led by Alex Turner, their ability to reinvent their sound keeps them at the forefront of modern rock. They rose to fame with their Album AM gaining massive popularity with songs like “Why do you only call me when you’re High” & “R U MINE”.

Scatter plot of Arctic Monkey Songs

library(ggplot2)
library(dplyr)

# Assuming df is your data frame with columns valence, energy, and album_name
df <- df |> 
  mutate(category = case_when(
    valence >= 0.5 & energy >= 0.5 ~ "Joyful",
    valence < 0.5 & energy >= 0.5 ~ "Angry",
    valence >= 0.5 & energy < 0.5 ~ "Peaceful",
    TRUE ~ "Sad"
  ))

# Define colors for categories
category_colors <- c("Joyful" = "gold", "Angry" = "red", "Peaceful" = "blue", "Sad" = "purple")

# Plotting the scatter plot with categories
ggplot(df, aes(x = valence, y = energy, color = category)) + 
  geom_point(size = 3, alpha = 0.7) +  # Adjust point size and transparency for clarity
  geom_hline(yintercept = 0.5, color = "grey", linetype = "dashed") +  # Horizontal separator
  geom_vline(xintercept = 0.5, color = "grey", linetype = "dashed") +  # Vertical separator
  scale_color_manual(values = category_colors) +  # Custom category colors
  labs(title = "Energy vs. Valence of Songs", 
       x = "Valence", 
       y = "Energy", 
       color = "Category") + 
  theme_light() +  # Cleaner theme
  theme(legend.position = "bottom", 
        axis.title = element_text(size = 12), 
        axis.text = element_text(size = 10), 
        plot.title = element_text(size = 14, face = "bold"))

The data shows that Arctic Monkeys have sung most of their songs in an Angry or Joyful tone. With Joyful songs taking an edge over their Angry tone songs!
